Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

1. Operator and scope

Dogs Page Publisher is a private content-publishing tool used only by authorized administrators of the Facebook Page ძაღლები - DOGS and its linked Instagram professional account @dzaghlebidogs.

The app does not offer public registration and does not collect personal data from Page visitors, followers, commenters or other members of the public.

2. Data processed

  • Meta Page, Instagram professional account and publication identifiers.
  • Captions, photos and videos approved for publication by an authorized administrator.
  • Meta-issued access tokens required to publish to the accounts.
  • Technical publication status, object identifiers, media checksums and sanitized audit events.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed solely to publish approved content, verify publication status, prevent accidental or altered publication, and maintain a minimal operational audit trail. The lawful basis is the authorized account administrator's instruction and legitimate interest in operating the accounts.

4. Sharing and sale

Data is not sold, rented or used for advertising profiles. It is shared only with Meta platforms as necessary to perform requested publishing actions and with infrastructure providers strictly required to run the tool.

5. Retention and security

Access tokens are stored locally in encrypted form using Windows Data Protection API protections and are retained until revoked, replaced or no longer required. Publication records are limited to operational metadata. Approved media remains under the operator's control. Secrets are not written to public logs.

6. User control and data deletion

An authorized administrator may revoke the app's access through Meta's business integration and security settings at any time.

To request deletion of data associated with Dogs Page Publisher, send a direct message to ძაღლები - DOGS and include the phrase Data deletion request: Dogs Page Publisher. The operator will verify the request, revoke or delete stored credentials and related operational records, and complete the request within 30 days unless retention is required by law or platform security requirements.

7. Terms of service

  • The tool may be used only by administrators authorized to manage the connected accounts.
  • Only approved content that the operator has the right to publish may be submitted.
  • Use must comply with Meta Platform Terms, Facebook and Instagram policies, and applicable law.
  • The tool is provided for the operator's internal publishing workflow and may be modified or discontinued.

8. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when the app's functionality or legal obligations change. Material updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date. Privacy and deletion questions may be sent by direct message through ძაღლები - DOGS.
